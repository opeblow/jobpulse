# JobPulse — Demo Video Script (under 3 minutes)

> **Why this wins:** A real everyday product (job tracking) where every sponsor does **real work on screen** — Firecrawl crawls, OpenAI generates, AgentMail sends and receives. We name each sponsor the moment it appears and sell **Convex depth** (live sync, webhook, actions) throughout.

- **Total runtime target:** ~2:35 (max 3:00)
- **Narration:** calm, confident. Pause between sections; let the crawl and webhook runs breathe on screen.
- **On screen:** a pre-populated board showing a *living* pipeline, with your AgentMail inbox open in a second tab.

---

## 0:00–0:10 — HOOK (10s)

**VO:** Meet JobPulse — it turns the chaos of the job hunt into a living board.

**Screen:** Landing with the JobPulse logo → click into the board.

---

## 0:10–0:35 — BOARD + ADD FROM URL (25s)

**VO:**
> My board — every application is a card in four columns: Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected.
> I paste a real posting URL and hit *Add job*. **Firecrawl** crawls it into clean markdown; **OpenAI** extracts company, role, location, salary, requirements, and drafts a checklist from the real posting. All on **Convex** — the card appears live.

**Screen:** Add job → paste URL → watched card populates in *Applied* with details + checklist.

---

## 0:35–1:15 — THE AI INBOX — AgentMail + OpenAI + real-time (40s)

**VO:**
> I forward every application email to my **AgentMail** inbox. When one lands, AgentMail fires a webhook, JobPulse's HTTP action catches it, and **OpenAI** classifies it — interview, offer, rejection, or follow-up.

**Screen:** AgentMail inbox tab → board → email lands → card slides to *Interview*, live.

**VO:**
> Watch that card jump from Applied to Interview — no drag, no refresh. That's **Convex** real-time.
> To follow up, I hit *AI draft reply* — **OpenAI** writes it, **AgentMail** sends it.

**Screen:** Card → *AI draft reply* → generated reply → *Send*.

---

## 1:15–1:50 — AI APPLICATION COACH — Firecrawl + OpenAI + Convex (35s)

**VO:**
> Hiring managers want tailored applications. The AI Coach makes that painless.
> **Firecrawl** researches the company; **OpenAI** blends it with the requirements into a fit score, the gaps to close, likely interview questions, and a prep plan. Persisted to **Convex**, updating live.

**Screen:** Open card → *AI Coach* → fit-score bar, coaching panel, company-intel section.

---

## 1:50–2:10 — ANALYTICS (10s)

**VO:**
> Because everything lives in Convex, the analytics build themselves — response, interview, offer, and conversion rates. I see exactly where my pipeline leaks and wins.

**Screen:** Analytics sidebar — response rate, interview rate, offer rate, conversion, average response.

---

## 2:10–2:35 — CLOSER + SPONSORS (25s)

**VO:**
> JobPulse is an everyday tool for the job hunt. Under the hood it's all **Convex** — schema, indexes, mutations, actions, a webhook, real-time sync — with **OpenAI** generating, **Firecrawl** crawling, and **AgentMail** sending and receiving email. Three sponsors doing real work on one reactive backend. Track every application to the offer — try JobPulse at the live link.

**Screen:** Pan across board + analytics → end on the JobPulse logo.

---

## Sponsor callout checklist (must appear on screen)

- [ ] **Firecrawl** — crawls the job posting URL (Add from URL)
- [ ] **Firecrawl** — crawls company intel (AI Coach)
- [ ] **OpenAI** — extracts job details + drafts checklist (Add from URL)
- [ ] **OpenAI** — classifies inbound emails (AI inbox webhook)
- [ ] **OpenAI** — drafts the follow-up reply (AI draft reply)
- [ ] **OpenAI** — fit score, gaps, interview prep (AI Coach)
- [ ] **AgentMail** — inbound inbox + webhook (receive)
- [ ] **AgentMail** — outbound sending (send reply)
- [ ] **Convex** — live re-render, webhook action, analytics, persistence (throughout)

## Timing guardrails

- Keep the cut under **3:00** — target **2:35**.
- Don't linger on any screen past ~15s.
- If you run long, trim the Coach or Analytics narration first; the board + AI inbox are the strongest moments.
